A calm notebook a clinician would respect.

One quiet serif for the moments that matter, a plain grotesque for everything else, warm-neutral paper, one deep green, hairline structure. This page is set in Steady's own tokens, so what you see is the design. Drawn from polarity.so, cohere.com and ada.cx.

Color

Swatches follow the theme switch above. Ratios are against the page background, except text on green.

There is no semantic red in the journal or the summary. Flagged entries use the green plus an icon and the word "Flagged". Red appears only on a real destructive action, like deleting the account.

From the references

Taken

  • Serif display over a grotesquePolarity: Exposure 48px, -3.36px tracking. Cohere: 96px hero serif.
  • Deep green and warm neutralsCohere's #39594d, #fafafa, #f0eee9, #212121.
  • Hairlines, no shadowsPolarity: 1px borders, zero box-shadows.
  • Pills for buttons and chipsCohere: 9999px radius. Ada: selected chip is a green tint with a green border.
  • Sage green for dark modeAda: #8fbe8e with dark text, 7.52:1 on slate #16242a (sampled).
  • 150ms ease on color and opacityPolarity and Cohere, same curve.

Left out

  • Coral and terracottaCohere #ff7759 is 2.61:1 on white. Both read as a warning.
  • Big stat tiles, live countersLarge symptom numbers would read as a score.
  • Food photographyPictures of food invite judging food.
  • Dark hero bands, looping animationUsers may open the app anxious or unwell.
  • Uppercase eyebrow labelsAda's tracked caps above headings. Plain sentence-case labels read calmer.
  • Italic emphasis wordsAda's "For real businesses". Reads as marketing, not clinical.
  • Glass panels, blurred photos, dark-firstAda's look. Steady takes only its colors, for dark mode.
